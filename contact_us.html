Delivery, returns and sizing

How long does delivery take?

We deliver within South Africa. Delivery usually takes 3 to 7 working days, and orders over R1,500 ship free.

Can I return or exchange something?

Unworn items with their tags can be exchanged within 14 days. Email us your order number and we will sort it out.

How do the sizes fit?

Our hoodies and tees are cut oversized. Take your usual size for the full look, or size down one for a closer fit.

Do you ship outside South Africa?

Not yet. Join the Icons and we will tell you first when we do.
